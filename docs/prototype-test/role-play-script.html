30-minute role-play

Practice with demo records only. Roles: staff member, family member, facilitator.

TimeScenarioActions and prompts
0–3 min Set up Facilitator assigns roles and a demo alias. Staff member creates a training episode. Family member opens the link. Ask: “What do you expect this page to tell you?”
3–10 min Routine procedure Facilitator says: “The next step in our exercise has begun.” Staff member advances one stage and shares the link. Family member explains the current stage and what happens next in their own words. Ask what is clear or uncertain.
10–18 min Delayed procedure Facilitator says: “The team is running behind in this exercise; no further stage change yet.” Staff member flags a delay. Family member refreshes or waits for the page update. Ask: “Does the wording feel calm? What would you do next?” Staff member clears the flag when prompted.
18–26 min Call the care team Facilitator says: “For this exercise, the family should speak directly with the care team.” Staff member sets the call-care-team status. Family member finds the message and tap-to-call option, then says what they would do. Do not place a real call.
26–30 min Debrief Staff member completes the short staff form; family member completes the family form. Facilitator asks each: “What took longest? What was confusing? What would you change first?” Record observations without names or patient details.

Facilitator reminders

  • Use only the training environment and invented details; stop if real patient information is entered.
  • Do not improvise clinical updates, diagnoses, results, or promises. Use only the scenario prompts and on-screen wording.
  • This is a usability exercise, not a care workflow. Follow local procedures for real communication.
